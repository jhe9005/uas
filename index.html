Tactical Aide-Mémoire — Small Arms C-sUAS

Field Operating Principles
Ground Troop Safety Rule

Maintain upper arm parallel to the ground or higher when in an air defence firing position so fire remains above horizontal and clear of friendly ground troops.

Diving UAS Aim Point

For a UAS diving directly onto your position without pulling up, aim directly at the center of the UAS (zero lead). Apply lead along the flight path if moving laterally/diagonally.

Rate & Engagement Technique

Use rapid single shots or ordered bursts. Swing through the target or hold a lead point ahead along the flight line depending on target geometry and weapon system.

GPMG & Shotgun Integration

Apply sustained/burst GPMG fire for area/volume denial and close-in L351A1 Mossberg 590M engagement within effective pattern range.
